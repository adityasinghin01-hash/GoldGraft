## Completion predicate
All required admitted scope is implemented and verified at actual target; no critical unresolved evidence/data/payment/notification failure; exact approved final artifact active; original/ongoing transaction obligations safe; merchant can sustain runtime/registry/state/secrets without GoldGraft temporary worker/grant; required exit cleanup acknowledged; no hidden manual merge/deploy/config/callback/data repair task. Current unknown external evidence => not Complete. Unsupported recurring/history obligations cannot become “not applicable” simply because first fixture lacks them.

User layer: “Your upgraded shop is running” only after predicate. Details: version, tested scope, deployment evidence, operational owner, recovery/support, remaining declared obligations and copy retention. “Sandbox checks passed”, “Review ready”, “Published”, “Deploying”, “Live checks pending” and “Cleanup pending” remain distinct. Necessary current controller/provider reviews/MFA may still appear at correct authority boundary, never repeated routine Accept prompts.

No promise a website runs forever or needs no future maintenance. The promise is qualified successful technical handover with durable merchant-controlled assets and explicit obligations, not disappearance of provider outages, costs or operational responsibility.
